## Brand voice

- **Direct.** Short sentences. No buzzwords ("synergy", "leverage", "solutions provider").
- **Honest.** No superlatives we can't defend. No "world-class" or "industry-leading."
- **Practical.** Aimed at shop owners, not engineers. Explain the benefit before the mechanism.
- **Confident, not pushy.** The CTA is direct ("Send last month's power bill via DM") but the rest of the copy assumes the reader is intelligent and busy.

## FAQ (seven questions)

1. **How do I know if my shop even has a power factor problem?**
   Check last bill. If PF < 0.95, losing money. Below 0.9, almost certainly paying a direct penalty.

2. **How much does an APFC panel cost?**
   Typical shop (5–25 kW): ₹35,000 – ₹1,50,000 depending on size. Payback 6–12 months.

3. **Will my shop have to shut down during installation?**
   No. 2–3 hours, scheduled around your hours (early morning or after close).

4. **What if I don't see savings?**
   Every install includes post-install verification. PF above 0.98 confirmed. Bill checked together.

5. **Does it need maintenance?**
   Almost none. Designed for 10+ years unattended. Annual visual check if you're in our service area.

6. **What happens when I add new equipment?**
   Handles normal growth automatically. For major additions, message us for an advisory.

7. **Is it safe? Monsoon, power cuts, lightning?**
   IP42/IP55, surge protection, MCCB isolation, fault cut-off. Built for Indian commercial conditions.
